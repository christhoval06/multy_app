## English Version

### App Store (iOS)

#### Promotional Text (Maximum 170 characters)
> Learn multiplication with personalized profiles, a redesigned study layout, and customizable study/quiz timers.

#### What’s New in This Version (Release Notes)
> **Multi-Profile Support & Guest Mode**
> - Create up to 4 distinct student profiles. Each profile tracks its own progress, best times, and session history independently.
> - Use the new temporary Guest Mode to practice without affecting saved statistics.
> - Existing progress is automatically migrated to your main profile upon opening the updated app.
> 
> **Refreshed Study Screen**
> - Redesigned math cards with a clean, game-inspired structure.
> - Clearer progress indicator tracking and easier-to-read layout components.
> - Polished layout showing interactive mathematical fun-facts at the bottom of your study session.
> 
> **Customizable Study & Quiz Timers**
> - Control your study speed with adjustable auto-advance options (3s, 5s, or 8s).
> - Adjust quiz challenge levels with set duration limits (30s, 50s, or 60s).
> - Added a "Restore Defaults" button in settings to easily reset timer configurations.

### Google Play Console (Android)

#### Short Description (Maximum 80 characters)
> Practice multiplication with custom user profiles, customizable timers, and a fresh UI.

#### What’s New (Release Notes)
> - **Multi-User Profiles:** Support for up to 4 personalized profiles to track individual progress and history, plus a temporary Guest Mode.
> - **Redesigned Study Screens:** Cleaner visualization of multiplication cards, clearer progress tracking, and interactive fun facts.
> - **Configurable Timers:** Set your preferred auto-advance speed for studying (3s, 5s, 8s) and time limits for quizzes (30s, 50s, 60s) directly from settings.
